For over 35 years, Mama Shiro has been perfecting hair and keeping the Gwachina community looking beautiful. From classic styles like Bandika to her absolute favourite — the Ethiopianth — she brings decades of skill and genuine care to every single customer.

Whether you are a student getting Lines for back-to-school, or a youth looking for a fresh new style — you are always welcome in Mama Shiro's chair.
